DMD Mouse pinout
----------------

1: +5V
2: vertical
3: vertical
4: horiz
5: horiz
6: gnd
7: middle button
8: right button
9: left button
A smallymouse can be connected directly (no inverters needed) and the DMD's 5V pin will power the smallymouse just fine.
