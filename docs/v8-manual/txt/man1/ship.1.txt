NAME
       ship - automatic software distribution

SYNOPSIS
       ship files

DESCRIPTION
       Ship distributes the named files to other computers, where they are
       installed under the same names.  The shipper must be registered in the
       tables of asd(5).  Ship will ask for a key to confirm the shipper's
       rights.

       Links among the named files are imitated on the receiving computer.
       Named files that do not exist on the sending computer are deleted on
       the receiving computer.

SEE ALSO
       asd(8), asd(5)
