NAME
       doctor, tso - psychiatric consultation

SYNOPSIS
       /usr/games/doctor

       /usr/games/tso

DESCRIPTION
       Doctor will understandingly explore most anything with you.  Just type
       your thoughts followed by double carriage returns.

       Tso, on the other hand, has a will of its own.
