NAME
       11as, 11cc, 11ld, 11nm, 11ranlib, 11reloc, 11size, 11strip - pdp11
       support

DESCRIPTION
       These programs do cross-compiling and related support functions for the
       DEC PDP-11 family of computers.  Their descriptions correspond closely
       with those of similarly named programs in Section 1 of this manual.
